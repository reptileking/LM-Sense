    How to judge
    Same
    One meaning, described twice. Different wording, a spelling variant, or “abbreviation of …” still counts if the meaning matches.
    Related
    Close but not interchangeable: one is broader or narrower, a plant and its fruit, a container and its contents, a process and its result, a group and a member, a literal use and a figurative one, a general sense and a technical one.
    Different
    Unrelated meanings that happen to share the word.
    Unsure
    You can't tell from what's shown. Use it sparingly; it counts as reviewed.
